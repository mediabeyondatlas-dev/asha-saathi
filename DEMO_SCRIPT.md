# ASHA Saathi — demo video script (2–3 minutes)

Record on phone, front camera, good light, clear voice. Screen-record the
app on the same phone (or a second phone showing the live app). English
narration — the app itself shows Hindi too. Total target: ~2:30.

---

## [0:00–0:20] Hook — to camera

> "In India, over one million ASHA workers walk village to village with a
> paper register and a pen. They are the first — often the only — health
> contact for hundreds of millions of people. But when a mother or a newborn
> is in danger, the worker has minutes to decide: refer today, or wait?
> This is ASHA Saathi — a field agent that helps her decide."

## [0:20–0:55] The problem + today's plan — screen: Dashboard

> "Every morning, the agent plans the day. Overdue vaccines first, then
> pregnant women due for check-ups, then open follow-ups. No searching
> through a paper register — the day is ranked for her."
>
> [Show: Dashboard with stats + Today's plan list.]

## [0:55–1:40] Triage — screen: Triage, pregnancy module

> "Here is the core. A guided interview — one question at a time. Is there
> bleeding? Severe headache? Fits? Any yes to a danger sign means one
> thing: refer now."
>
> [Tap through 2–3 questions, answer YES to "Severe headache". Show the
> red REFER NOW banner.]
>
> "The app names the facility, and writes the referral slip — ready to copy
> to WhatsApp or print. The visit is logged automatically."
>
> [Show: referral slip + copy button.]

## [1:40–2:10] Vaccines + language — screen: Vaccines tab, then toggle Hindi

> "It also tracks every child's immunisation from their date of birth —
> due, overdue, one tap to mark given. And it speaks her language — one tap
> switches the whole app to Hindi."
>
> [Show: due-list with an overdue vaccine, mark one given. Toggle to Hindi.]

## [2:10–2:30] Close — to camera

> "No internet needed. No account. No data leaves her phone — it works
> fully offline, because that's where she works. ASHA Saathi: a field agent
> for the million workers holding up India's health. Try it at the link
> below."
